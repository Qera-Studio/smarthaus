## 6. The design review

A page-by-page critique on 2026-11-17, from screenshots of every page at 1440px and on an iPhone 17. The verdict: the structure is premium and the fill is not. The glass hero, the isometric drawings and the Works With wiring are agency-grade; the photography and a handful of borrowed patterns pull the rest down to a contractor's site. It looks cheap where slots were filled with default patterns instead of designed from the brand's own idea. Work the tables in order: the wrong pictures first, then the system, then each page.

### What to keep, and design the rest from

The glass hero, the isometric drawings, the Works With wiring, the warm palette, most of the copy (specific and calm), the FAQ and legal layout, the nav capsule and the Solutions menu. These are the parts designed from the brand's own idea. The fixes above bring everything else up to them.

### The order

1. Pull the wrong pictures.
2. Settle one picture language: drawings and villa renders in one warm grade.
3. One heading system, the article's line length, no brackets.
4. Packages in place of software pricing.
5. Coming-soon pages out of the menus, particle text gone.
6. Cut the effects to two signatures.
7. The footer.
